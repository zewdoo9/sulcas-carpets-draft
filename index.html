Our story

From the Bay Area to Elk Grove — a family showroom since 2003

Sulca’s Carpets (Sulca’s Carpets, Inc. — also known as Sulcas Carpets & more) is a family- and veteran-owned flooring showroom serving Elk Grove and the surrounding area. The business put down roots here in 2003 after beginnings in the Bay Area, and has grown into a roughly 6,000 square-foot space where homeowners can see and feel materials before they decide.

Owner Rafael Sulca leads with a hands-on approach. Many customers find Sulca’s through referrals — friends and neighbors who liked the craftsmanship, the polite crews, and the care taken from estimate to install. Free in-home estimates help you plan with real measurements and samples in your space.

Estimates are often associated with Cain Cazarez, who helps homeowners walk through options clearly and fairly. Whether you’re refreshing a single room or tackling kitchen and bath remodel, the team is set up for flooring and finishes — not carpet cleaning.
