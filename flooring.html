Flooring Options in Elk Grove & Sacramento

Not sure which floor fits your home? Here’s a straightforward look at the flooring we install — what each one is best for and why homeowners choose it. Come see and feel every option in our Elk Grove showroom, or we’ll bring samples to you with a free in-home estimate.

Carpet

Soft and quiet underfoot, carpet is still the most comfortable way to finish a bedroom or family room. Our showroom wall lets you compare colors, textures and pile heights side by side, and we install it for bedrooms, stairs and living spaces.

Best for: Bedrooms, family rooms, stairs, home offices

  • Warm, cushioned feel and a quieter home
  • Huge range of colors and textures to compare in person
  • Installed on stairs as well as full rooms
Get a free estimate

Hardwood

Real wood brings warmth and character that carries from room to room. We install classic and modern hardwood looks — including Hallmark Floors hardwoods on display in our showroom — and offer refinishing and staining to bring existing floors back.

Best for: Living rooms, dining rooms, hallways, stairs, bedrooms

  • Natural grain and a timeless look
  • Can be refinished and re-stained instead of replaced
  • Pairs with matching stair treads for a finished look
Get a free estimate

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)

Luxury vinyl plank gives you a wood look in a hard-wearing plank that stands up to busy households. Waterproof options make it a go-to for kitchens, baths and open floor plans where you want one continuous floor.

Best for: Kitchens, bathrooms, laundry rooms, open-concept living areas

  • Waterproof options for spill-prone rooms
  • Realistic wood looks in many tones
  • Easy everyday cleaning for kids and pets
Get a free estimate

Laminate

Laminate delivers a wood-look surface with a tough top layer at a friendly price point. It’s a practical choice for high-traffic rooms when you want the look of hardwood without the upkeep.

Best for: Living rooms, hallways, bedrooms, rentals and busy family spaces

  • Budget-friendly wood look
  • Durable surface for everyday traffic
  • Wide choice of plank colors and finishes
Get a free estimate

Tile & Natural Stone

Ceramic, porcelain and natural stone are made for floors and wet areas. From entry floors to full walk-in showers, tile is a natural fit for kitchen and bath projects — American Olean is among the brands in our showroom.

Best for: Bathrooms, showers, kitchens, entryways, laundry rooms

  • Made for wet areas and showers
  • Ceramic, porcelain and natural stone choices
  • Easy to clean and very long-lasting
Get a free estimate

Area Rugs

An area rug is the finishing piece that ties a room together — adding warmth, color and softness on top of hardwood, LVP or tile. Pick one out alongside your new floors so everything works together.

Best for: Living rooms, bedrooms, dining areas, over hard floors

  • Adds warmth and softness to hard floors
  • Defines seating and dining areas
  • An easy way to bring in color and texture
